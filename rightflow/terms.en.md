## 1. App capabilities and versions

RightFlow provides a short macOS Finder menu and lets you configure menu order, switches, templates, a terminal, an editor, and saved locations. File operations take place locally, with access limited by system authorization and file permissions.

Free and Pro features depend on the app's actual descriptions and entitlement status. Current free everyday features include paths, names, creating text or Markdown files, one terminal selection, and one saved location. Cut / Paste, custom templates, additional saved locations, and an editor are Pro features. The current development build includes 7zz local archive extraction; availability and supported formats depend on the installed version. Development validation is not an App Store release.

Plans, prototypes, or features being integrated in the documentation do not indicate that those features have been released and do not promise unlimited format support, full-disk access, or that all future features will be free forever.

## 3. File operations and responsible use

Only process files that you are authorized to access, and comply with applicable copyright, confidentiality obligations, and legal requirements. RightFlow grants no new rights to third-party files.

Creating files, moving items, Cut / Paste, and extraction change local files or folders. Back up important material before operating on it, and confirm the destination and available disk space. Disabling a menu action does not undo completed file operations.

Damaged or encrypted archives, invalid authorization, disconnected external drives, system protections, and limitations of external apps may cause operations to fail. The app aims to provide error feedback but cannot guarantee that every external file, system version, or third-party app will always work. If something goes wrong, stop repeating the operation, check the state of your files, and contact support.

These precautions do not remove the provider's responsibilities under applicable law or transfer responsibility for all failures to the user.
